P0Security

1. Any URL ending in “.png” skips sign-in, and the app then trusts a user ID sent by the browser

What
proxy.js skips every path matching .*\.png$. The proxy is also what strips the x-tas-user header, so on those paths a forged header reaches sessionUserId() untouched. Pages such as /admin/anything.png still route to app/admin/[master], and Server Actions posted to that URL run as whoever the header names.
Proof
On localhost: /admin/customers.png with no header → 307 to /login. Same URL with an admin's ID in x-tas-user → passed requireRole('admin') and rendered the admin shell. User IDs are not secret (the salesperson picker carries them as option values). The live site runs the same code. I did not test it.
Fix
Remove .*\.png$ from the matcher and list the real image files by name (letterhead.png, the app icons). Also make sessionUserId() trust the header only when the proxy marks it with a server secret, so a future matcher change can't reopen this. About 5 lines.

proxy.js:41 · lib/server.js:30

P1Wrong results

2. Plan “Auto-issue RM to line” can see only 1,000 of 2,203 materials

What
autoIssuePlan reads materials with a plain select('*'), and Supabase returns at most 1,000 rows. The plan dialog loads the full list through invMasters(), so the screen shows every material matched while the issue skips most of them.
Proof
BOM run for all 3,040 products: with every material loaded, every line finds a material. With the first 1,000 rows only, all 4 core granules, both adhesives, all 10 protective films, 8 bottom coils and 160 top coils go unmatched. Auto-issue then moves only part of the material, or stops with “No raw material could be matched”.
Fix
Use (await invMasters()).materials in autoIssuePlan. One line.

app/production/actions.js:249

3. No 1510 mm “SUPER HIGH SS10+” protective film in Materials

What
The protective film level comes from the colour, and 1500 mm panels need a 1510 mm film. Materials has 1510 mm film only in LOW, MEDIUM and HIGH, for both brands. Three of the 11 real products are 1500 mm in SUPER HIGH colours, so their DPR shows no film material and auto-issue silently leaves the film out.
Why
The Excel sheet has 1500 mm products in only 5 colours, all MEDIUM, so it never needed the other films. Two ways to fix it: add PROTECTIVE FILM-ALCLAD-0.080*1510-SUPER HIGH SS10+ and the ALUMAX one to Materials, or decide that 1500 mm always uses MEDIUM film. You need to choose.

Materials data · ALUMAX-AL4045-AL4004-FRB1-0.4-1500*2000, ALCLAD-AL4035-AL4002-NFR-0.3-1500*2450, ALCLAD-AL4035-AL4002-FRB1-0.3-1500*2000

4. “Undo dispatch” no longer fits the Ship flow: the same pieces can be shipped and invoiced twice

What
Ship now makes the DO and the dispatch in one step. Undo (order dialog, plant role) runs the old undo_dispatch: it deletes the dispatch row and lowers dispatched_qty, but the DO batch line, any PI on it and the cleared ready pieces stay as they were. The pieces go back into the balance and can be shipped on a new DO and invoiced again.
Fix
Either remove Undo, or turn it into “Cancel DO” that also removes the batch line, refuses when a PI exists and puts the ready pieces back.

P2Edge cases

5. FR-A2 products get the wrong adhesive and film width or brand

What
Products outside the standard (FR-A2, other thicknesses) use the formula, and its adhesive and film come from the fixed material on the core settings row. Width and brand are ignored. Real example: ALCLAD FR-A2 1500 mm gets ADHESIVE 0.080*1280 and PROTECTIVE FILM-ALUMAX-0.080*1260-HIGH.
Fix
Match adhesive and film by width and brand on the formula path, the same way the standard path already does.

lib/bom.js findMatchingMaterial / calcPanelBOM formula branch

6. Grade and Al skin are picked separately and can disagree

What
A quote line picks Grade and Al Skin Thickness from separate lists. The standard BOM follows the grade (AL4045 = 0.38 mm top skin), so an item saved as ALUMAX-AL4045-AL3003-FRB1-0.5 is costed and issued as 0.38 mm while its name says 0.5.
Fix
Fill skin from the grade on the quote form, or refuse a skin that doesn't match the grade.

app/orders/new/QuoteForm.js · app/orders/actions.js parseLines

7. PI: a refusal from the database is ignored and the PI is written anyway

What
Ship shows any error from generate_batch_do. PI falls back to direct table writes on any error from generate_batch_pi, not only when the function is missing. The batch is also not checked to belong to the order in the form.
Fix
Use the same rule as Ship, and check batch.order_id.

app/orders/actions.js:428

8. Ship checks the balance outside the database lock and does not need ready pieces

What
“Not more than the balance” is checked in the app before calling generate_batch_do, so two Ship submits at the same moment can both pass and over-ship. The function also accepts pieces that were never marked ready (ready just floors at 0).
Fix
Check qty ≤ qty − dispatched_qty inside the function after its for update. Decide whether Ship must stay within ready pieces.

supabase/migrate-017.sql generate_batch_do
